# Aceapp voluntary support

Aceapp's Support page is donation-only. Donations do not unlock features and are not required for access.

## Public routes

- PayPal hosted support page: `https://www.paypal.com/ncp/payment/23PT4EVR3Q34C`
- BPI / InstaPay QR: `assets/bpi-instapay-qr.png`

## Privacy choice

The public site intentionally does **not** display a personal mobile number or full bank-account number. For bank transfers, the donor scans the BPI/InstaPay QR and verifies the recipient inside their own banking app before confirming.

## Removed

- Xendit checkout
- Xendit Netlify function
- GCash public number / QR
- payment confirmation or proof upload
- donation-to-access entitlements

This is personal voluntary support and should not be described as a charitable or tax-deductible donation.
